## Core idea

Lower token cost changes the floor, but user experience, reliability, and task completion rate still decide who captures the value.

## Recall bullets

1. Buyers compare outcomes, not unit economics in isolation.
2. Fast feedback loops matter more when agents chain multiple calls.
3. Distribution advantage can outweigh raw model pricing.

## Why save it

This is the kind of piece you may want to revisit later when evaluating product strategy rather than model capability alone.
